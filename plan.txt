- Confirmed OAuth scope enforcement defects in oauth.controller.ts: /userinfo returned the subject even without the required openid scope, and /businesses allowed any openid token to enumerate the user's verified businesses without business:read. Added explicit openid and business:read checks.
- Confirmed MFA recovery-code race: login and MFA-disable flows previously read unused codes and then unconditionally updated usedAt, allowing two concurrent requests to consume the same recovery code. Changed both paths to conditional updateMany (usedAt IS NULL) and require exactly one row to be consumed.
